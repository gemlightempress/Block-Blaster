Privacy Policy

What Block Blaster: Kingdom of Block uses, why, and what choices you have about it.

Last updated: November 2026

Overview

Block Blaster: Kingdom of Block is developed and published by Gemlight Empress and played on Facebook as an Instant Game. This Privacy Policy explains what information the game uses, how it is used, and the choices available to you. The game asks you for no extra permissions: it never asks for your friends list, email address or any other Facebook permission.

Information We Use

Game Data

To save your progress, the game stores the following in the Instant Games player data that Facebook keeps for this game, so it is there the next time you play, on any device you use Facebook on:

  • Your best score
  • Your chosen block design and board size
  • Achievements
  • Today’s Crunch streak
  • The display name you choose and a random Player ID created by the game (it is only shown on your profile, so you can quote it to support)
  • Whether you have completed (or skipped) the first-time tutorial
  • Sound, music and vibration settings

This data is saved with Facebook and is not sent to any server run by us. We do not receive it and we cannot see it.

Your Facebook Name and Picture

Instant Games lets a game show a player’s name and picture without asking for any permission. If Facebook provides them, the game may show your Facebook name on your in-game profile until you type a name of your own. They stay on your device while you play and are not collected or stored by us.

Sharing and Invites

The Share button opens Facebook’s own Invite Friends and Share Game dialogs with a picture and a short message. You choose who receives it, and nothing is sent unless you confirm in Facebook’s dialog. We receive no list of your friends and nothing is given in return for sharing.

Advertising

The game is free to play and shows advertisements provided by Meta Audience Network through Facebook Instant Games:

  • A banner advertisement in a reserved strip at the bottom of the screen. The game sits above it and the banner never covers the board.
  • Full-screen advertisements between games, for example after a game ends.
  • Optional rewarded video advertisements that you choose to watch for a bonus: continuing your game when the board is full (Revive). The bonus is given only if you watch the video to the end.

Meta provides, shows and measures these advertisements. The game does not give advertisers any information about you. How Meta uses information for advertising, and the choices you have about ads, are explained in Meta’s Privacy Policy and in your Facebook ad preferences.

What the Game Does Not Do

  • It does not run its own analytics, and it does not use Firebase or Google services.
  • It does not collect your email address, phone number, contacts or location.
  • It does not use cookies of its own or place tracking code in the game.
  • There are no in-app purchases in this version of the game.

Third-Party Services

The game runs inside Facebook and uses the following service, which is governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Facebook Instant Games (Meta Platforms) — running the game, saving your progress, the Invite Friends and Share Game dialogs, and the Meta Audience Network advertisements.

Children’s Privacy

The game is designed for a general audience. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data Retention & Deletion

You can erase your game progress at any time with “Reset Progress” in Settings, which replaces your saved game data with a fresh start. Data that Facebook holds about you is kept and deleted according to your Facebook account settings and Meta’s policies.

Your Choices

  • You can turn off sound, music and vibration in Settings.
  • You can change your display name at any time from your profile in Settings.
  • You can erase your game progress at any time using “Reset Progress” in Settings.
  • You can manage ad preferences and the data Facebook holds about you in your Facebook settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the game or applicable law. When we make changes, we will update the Last Updated date above.
