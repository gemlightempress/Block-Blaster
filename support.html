Frequently Asked Questions for Block Blaster: The Kingdom of Blocks.

How do I play?
Drag any of the three pieces in your tray onto the board. Fill an entire row or column and it clears. There's no time limit — the round ends when none of your three current pieces fit anywhere.
How is my best score saved?
Your best score is saved automatically on your device, and backed up to Google Play Games Cloud Save while you're automatically signed in. Playing on another device with the same Google account brings your best score with you.
Do I need to sign in manually?
No. The Game signs in automatically using the Google account already active on your device — there's no login screen or button. If no account is available, the Game works exactly the same, just without the score backup.
What does the revive option do?
When you run out of moves, you may be offered a one-time revive for that round — watch a short rewarded video ad, and two of your most-filled lines are cleared so you can keep playing.
The game is running slowly or lagging. What should I do?
Close other apps, restart the Game, and make sure your device has enough free memory.
How do I report a bug or problem?
Contact support with your device model, Game version, and a description of the problem. Screenshots are helpful.
