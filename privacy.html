Privacy Policy

What Block Blaster: The Kingdom of Blocks collects, why, and what choices you have about it.

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Overview

Block Blaster: The Kingdom of Blocks is developed and published by Gemlight Empress. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Game collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

  • Locally stored game data: your board size preference, block theme selection, and sound/music/vibration settings are stored on your device and are not transmitted to our servers.
  • Google Play Games sign-in (automatic): the Game automatically signs in using the Google account already active on your device — there is no manual login screen. When signed in, Google Play Games Services accesses your Google player identifier to back up your best score, governed by Google's own privacy policy. If sign-in isn't available or is skipped, the Game remains fully playable using local storage only.
  • Advertising identifiers: if advertising is enabled (Google AdMob), the ad network may collect device advertising identifiers, coarse location, and interaction data to serve and measure ads, subject to your device's ad-tracking settings.

How We Use Information

  • To save your game progress and settings locally on your device.
  • To back up your best score to Google Play Games Cloud Save while signed in (see "Cloud Save & Score Sync" below).
  • To display and measure advertising through Google AdMob.

Third-Party Services

The Game integrates the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play Games Services (automatic sign-in, best score Cloud Save)
  • Google AdMob (advertising)

Children's Privacy

The Game is designed to be suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is required for functionality such as advertising, which is configured to respect applicable child-directed treatment settings (e.g., Google's Families Ad Program) when the app is listed for a young audience.

Cloud Save & Score Sync

Your best score is saved on your device and, while automatically signed in, backed up to Google Play Games Cloud Save. Playing on another device with the same Google account restores your best score there — whichever score is higher is kept as the active one. If you play without an internet connection, your score continues saving locally and syncs the next time you're online.

Data Retention & Deletion

Locally stored progress and settings can be erased at any time by clearing app data/storage through your device settings, or by uninstalling the Game. Cloud-saved score data associated with your Google Play account is retained and deleted according to your Google Account settings.

Your Choices

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings.
  • You can independently disable sound, music, and vibration in the in-game Settings screen.
  • Because sign-in is automatic and tied to your device's active Google account, you can prevent it by signing out of Google Play Games at the device level, or managing permissions through your Google Account settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes to the Game or applicable law.
